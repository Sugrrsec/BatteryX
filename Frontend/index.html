How to read the results

State of health is the latest capacity divided by the first recorded capacity. A file starting mid-life gives health relative to that starting point.

Remaining life extends the average capacity loss per cycle to the 80% threshold. Flat or rising capacity yields no estimate.

Temperature, voltage and chemistry come from the specimen record. Model inference and calibrated uncertainty belong to the trained-model workflow.
